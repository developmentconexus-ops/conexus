# Faturamento

- `periodosDeFaturamento`: the monthly billing periods Mercado Livre charges the seller: key, dates and amounts.
- `cobrancasDoPeriodo`: the charges of one period, by its key.
- `notaFiscalDoPedido`: the invoice issued for an order: series, number, status and date. The recipient and their CPF or CNPJ are not in the answer.

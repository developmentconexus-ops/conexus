# The card of a screen

Read only when the step creates or changes a screen.

1. Compare the screen's prototype with the study's captures of the old product, state by state
   ([study screens](../../conexus-study/references/screens.md)).
2. The card carries the images: the old shots the prototype follows and the box layouts at 1280 and
   390 px, from the two or three of different organisation the study made.
3. The summary of the `shape` interview, phase 3, becomes the card's screens part. The interview's
   questions went to the founder through firstmate in Portuguese, never by Impeccable's own
   interview ([delivery L23](../../../../docs/guides/delivery.md)).
4. Use `onboard` when the step has a first time, and fill the matrix's "primeira vez" and "sem
   permissão" columns for every screen.
5. A finding of Impeccable never blocks alone; [delivery L12](../../../../docs/guides/delivery.md)
   judges it. Which commands, hooks and configuration Conexus allows is owned by
   [Impeccable](../../../../docs/reference/impeccable.md).

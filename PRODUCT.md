# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People in Brazilian companies, writing in Portuguese, who are not developers: company owners and
administrators who set the company up, and members who ask for and use apps. One founder operates the
installation through the Central. Owner: docs/architecture.md §1 "Who has a stake".

## Product Purpose

A person asks the Builder, in Portuguese, for a business app; Conexus builds, previews, publishes and
runs it for that company, on that company's data. Owner: docs/architecture.md §1.

## Positioning

Creation by conversation, real code, official data stays in the company's systems. Not an ERP, a
database console or a drag-and-drop tool. Owner: docs/architecture.md §1 "What the product never does".

## Capabilities and Constraints

The roadmap owns what exists: docs/roadmap.md. Web rules: docs/guides/web.md (W21 shadcn on Base UI,
W22 strict CSP, no CDN fonts). Design rules: docs/guides/design.md (V17 no inline style, V18 390 px and
200% zoom). Visible text is Portuguese; everything else English (delivery L23). Questions for the
founder go through firstmate, never a direct interview.

## Brand Commitments

"Grafite e Ipê" and the Encaixe mark, as DESIGN.md (docs/guides/design.md) records. Voice: V39.

## Evidence on Hand

No customer yet. Never invent testimonials, customers, numbers or claims.

## Product Principles

It never invents a value or simulates progress. It never lets the model declare work done. It never
overwrites someone else's newer work. It never shows a credential. It never publishes on its own.

docs/guides/design.md V8, V18, V28 to V31.

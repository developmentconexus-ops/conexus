# Envios

- `envio`: one shipment in the new format (status, substatus, order, tracking, logistic). The destination (`destination`: the address, and the name and phone of who receives) is in the answer for whoever may see addresses.
- `prazoDoEnvio`: the dispatch deadline (SLA) of a shipment.
- `estoqueFull`: the Full stock of one inventory: total, available and not available by reason.

# Design system: Conexus Hub, "Noite e ipê"

This file owns the values and the pieces; the [design guide](docs/guides/design.md) owns the rules,
and a rule is cited here by its identifier. The front matter's values are the ones
`apps/web/src/brand/tokens.css` holds, name by name (`brand/tokens.test.ts`). A mark **(target)**
names a piece, or a part of one, that the code does not have yet.

## Overview

**Noite e ipê, in graphite** (direction B; founder, 2026-11-26: "Fecha"). The Hub is an Operate
surface: a company owner or administrator comes to ask about the company's data, pick up a
conversation and see what is built and what broke. The screen is a lit sheet of work set in a dark
frame, the way a desk lamp cuts a page out of a room at night, and the one warm colour on it is the
ipê.

Two regions (V19). The frame (the sidebar and the margin around the sheet) is graphite in both
themes; the sheet sits inset in it with a 16px corner, near white in the light theme and one step
above the frame in the dark one. Graphite has no hue, so ipê is the only colour of the screen;
green, orange and red are held for state, and violet for the memory budget. Ipê is a lit fill with
dark ink on it, and only on what is on: the primary button, a switch and a checkbox that are on,
progress and send (V5). An edge is a shadow, not a hairline: what is white draws no border, and in
the dark theme each layer takes a thread of light along its top.

- One 4px base unit: every height, line height, gap and inset is a multiple of 4 (V16).
- One row pitch (V58): 32px on a pointer and 44px on touch, for every list, so lists side by side
  line up.
- One axis (V59): the greeting, composer, Continuar and the Projetos | Ligações pair share one centre
  line and one width.
- One family, Hanken Grotesk, for display and UI; JetBrains Mono only for facts (V11, V23).
- A state is a word, with at most a dot of its colour beside it (V35).

## Colors

A graphite ramp with no hue and one warm colour. A token has a light and a dark value; the dark one
is written once, with the `dark-` prefix above. A colour resolves where it is used, so a piece
inside the frame takes its dark value in both themes.

an icon.

## Typography

One grotesque for everything: one x-height and one rhythm from greeting to row label. Hanken Grotesk
is self-hosted in `apps/web/src/brand/fonts`, covers weights 400 to 700 in one variable file and has
the tabular figures the stamp and every count use. Bricolage Grotesque, the previous display face,
is retired (V23). The display title is 30/36 at 700, and 24/32 below 640px (V13). Every line height
sits on the 4px grid. There are nine text tokens: seven Hanken
sizes, `display-narrow` as the form of `display` below 640px (V13), and the mono `fact`.

| Token | Where |
| --- | --- |
| `display`, `display-narrow` | the Início greeting, one per page |
| `heading` | a page title on a list or settings page |
| `title` | header bar, dialog and drawer titles, the company name |
| `reading` | long message prose |
| `body` | UI text, the composer field, bubbles, tables |
| `label` | rows, buttons, menus, chips; 600 on block titles |
| `eyebrow` | group labels ("Hoje", "Esta semana"), ages, stamps, menu section heads |
| `fact` | mono: timestamps, e-mail, exact values to copy (V11) |

Every figure that can change (ages, counts, versions, percentages) is set with tabular figures
(V23). The `eyebrow` labels a group or a fact; it never announces a title (V61).

## Layout

**Frame, from 768px.** The rail (`sidebar-icon`) and the list (`list`) are together the `sidebar`:
fixed, on the frame's graphite in both themes, with a hairline between the two. Closed, only the
rail stays. The rest is the sheet, inset in the frame: `sm` (8px) of frame above, below and at its
right, the `card` corner, and a ring for its edge (V19, W30). Below 768px the sheet takes the whole
screen, with no margin and no corner.

- **Rail.** The mark tile and the place tiles are `rail-tile` with the rail icon size (V34), `xs`
  apart; Início, Projetos, Integrações at the top, then Automações right after Integrações (only for
  an owner or an administrator; founder, 2026-11-02, "Opção 1"); Configurações, the bell just above
  the account, and the account (`avatar`) at the foot. The current tile takes the lighter fill (the
  frame with 14% of white) and its icon the ipê; no tick (V5).
- **List.** The company switcher, "Nova conversa" (a default button), then "Conversas" in day groups
  (Hoje, Ontem, Esta semana, Antes) as `eyebrow` rows and conversation rows of `spacing.row` with the
  age at the end. The current row takes the same lighter fill, its text in `text` and its icon, when
  it has one, in ipê.
- **Header bar.** Title centred, bottom hairline, desktop only. **Phone top bar:** the desk's
  ground, bottom hairline, the safe-area inset: menu, mark, place title. While a notification is unread the menu
  button carries a small `danger` dot, the colour of the bell's count (ipê marks no attention, it
  is held for what is on and the current place), and its name adds the count (founder, 2026-11-02,
  "2B", emergência).
- **The axis (V59).** `axis`, and `axis-wide` from 1600px wide. A secondary block is `half`: the
  axis less the gutter, divided by two. In a conversation the transcript, notices and composer share
  the axis.
- **Padding.** Bounded pages take `xl` inline and `2xl` block from 768px, `lg` and `xl` below. The
  Início stack: `3xl` between sections, `xl` between greeting and composer. Full-height pages take
  no frame padding.
- **Spacing scale.** The `spacing` tokens, with `row` as the list pitch (V58). Widths never reuse
  these names (V23).

| Width | What changes |
| --- | --- |
| a box below 320px | the composer row wraps: the left group takes its own line |
| a box below 416px | the composer row drops words, never controls; measured on the box, so the Builder's narrow box too (V60) |
| below 640px | `display` becomes `display-narrow` |
| below 768px (390 reference) | the rail and the list move together into the left drawer, graphite as the frame (V56); the sheet is not inset; no header bar; the "+" and Projeto menus become bottom drawers; Início blocks stack in one column; rows are 44 on touch |
| 768px and up | rail and list fixed; popovers; Projetos and Ligações side by side |
| 1600px and up | the axis widens to `axis-wide` |

## Elevation & Depth

An edge is a shadow (V19). Every shadow starts with the same 1px ring, 10% of ink in the light theme
and 9% of white in the dark one, and in the dark theme ends with a thread of light inside the top
edge. Overlays (sheet, dialog, drawer backdrop) dim with the desk at 80%, not black.

| Shadow | For |
| --- | --- |
| `ring` | the edge alone: the inset sheet, the avatar, the switch's off track, the checkbox's off box |
| `1` | a white piece at rest: button, field, card, badge, table frame |
| `2` | the same piece under the mouse |
| `float` | what floats over the sheet: menu, popover, select, combobox, dialog, drawer, side panel |
| `composer` | the composer only |

The lit ipê carries its own gloss: a light line inside its top, a darker one inside its foot and a
warm 1px ring, and under the mouse it brightens and casts a short warm glow.

**Motion.** One sheet ([movimento](docs/reference/movimento.md)); `brand/motion.css` writes each
name below once, and a piece takes the name instead of a number of its own (V26). Two still write
a number: the message scroller's button and the memory status in the room.

| Name | Value | For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `duration-row` | 75ms | the colour of a sidebar, menu or palette row; a menu's or tooltip's exit |
| `duration-control` | 160ms | the colour and shadow of a button, tab, table row, field or card; a transition that names no time |
| `duration-press` | 420ms | the press: a button gives to 96% and comes back |
| `duration-state` | 560ms | a switch or a checkbox changing state |
| `duration-menu` | 120ms | a menu, popover, select, combobox or tooltip entering |
| `duration-window` | 150ms | a dialog and its veil; the exit of a drawer, side panel or toast |
| `duration-move` | 200ms | a drawer, side panel or toast entering |
| `duration-page` | 160ms | a change of screen |
| `ease-enter` | `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` | what enters |
| `ease-exit` | `ease-in` | what leaves |
| `ease-fit` | `cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)` | what moves without entering or leaving: the mark |
| `ease-spring-press` | `linear()` spring | the press |
| `ease-spring-state` | `linear()` spring, calmer | the change of state |

The same file names the two movements that pieces share: `button-motion` (the press and the mouse
on a button) and `popup-motion` (a popup entering and leaving).

What moves, and how:

- **The mouse** changes colour and shadow only. **A press** gives to 96% and comes back. **A switch
  or a checkbox** moves its colour and its thumb. **Focus** never moves: the ring shows at once.
- **Menu, popover, select, combobox** enter by opacity and scale from 96% to 100%, from their
  trigger, and leave faster. A select that opens over its trigger, as it does under a mouse or the
  keyboard, is there at once. **Tooltip** the same entry, and passing from one tooltip to the next
  opens the second with no transition.
- **Dialog**: opacity only, of the window and its veil. **Drawer and side panel**: a short slide in
  and out; no transition while a finger drags.
- **A change of screen**, every change of the address's path, from the sidebar or after an action,
  fades only the sheet; the sidebar does not blink. With a drawer or a window open the screen changes
  at once, and they leave in their own time. A page's load, a change of the address's search alone
  (the sidebar's search and tab) and a change that arrives during a fade apply at once too.
- **Toast**, only for what went well: it enters by a short rise and opacity, leaves by opacity and
  goes away by itself; its height never animates, so the ones under it take its place at once.
- **AI pieces and tables** move only under the mouse. **Skeleton** keeps its pulse.
- **At once**: the sidebar collapsing, a folding panel (tool line, reasoning, group), a new message
  and arriving text (only the scroll to the end is smooth), a row entering or leaving a list, a page
  turning ready on the first-entry panel. Tab pill, rolling number, drawn tick and a button's
  "doing" and "done" stay at once until their pieces are born.
- **Never**: errors and validation, focus, width, height, margin or layout position, a page's load,
  and a loop that is not work in progress.
- **Ambient**, only while work runs: a busy composer's ring turns in 2.8s, working text shimmers in
  1.8s, the mark's pieces part and fit back in 1.2s (`ease-fit`); the mark arrives in 0.9s
  (`ease-enter`).

Under `prefers-reduced-motion` nothing animates, not even a fade: every duration is zero and every
loop stops, with the final state readable.

## Shapes

Rounded, a small set (V22): `control`, 10px (buttons, inputs, rows, rail tiles, tooltips), `object`,
14px (popovers, menus, dialogs, bubbles), `card`, 16px (cards, the inset sheet's corner, a bottom
drawer's top corners), `composer`, 22px, `pill` (chips, switches, soft buttons, send, badges),
`phone` (the Prévia's drawn phone only). The checkbox is a 20px box with a 7px corner, since
`control` would make it a circle. Regions (rail, list, header bar) take none.

**Edges.** A white piece has no border: its edge is the ring of its shadow (V19). 1px `line` is for
a rule between rows or blocks inside one piece, and between the rail and the list.

**Icons** (V34). lucide-react, line icons, sized by the neighbour: the rail, a label, a toolbar or
small button, and a smaller one for the "→" of a block's way out. A connector's logo sits on the
`logo-plate`: 24px plate and 16px logo in menus, 20 and 14 on rows and in the tools count. No glyph
or emoji icons.
